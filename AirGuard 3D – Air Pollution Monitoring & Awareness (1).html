About AirGuard 3D

AirGuard 3D is a school and hackathon project that explains air pollution, shows how air quality is read, and helps people act. Demo data is always labelled. Reports and subscriptions are stored in the page’s built-in shared database. The Node/Express + SQLite version from the project brief is not part of this hosted page.
